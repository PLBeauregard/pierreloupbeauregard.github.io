Pierre-Loup Beauregard

Assistant Professor, Department of Economics, Université de Montréal

I am a labour economist. My research focuses primarily on income inequality and the effects of public policy on poverty, with a particular interest in social housing and homelessness. I am also interested in labour unions and labour market discrimination.
